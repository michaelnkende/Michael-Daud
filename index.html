Profile

Professional profile

Michael Lucas Daud is an Assistant Lecturer in the Department of Agricultural Sciences at Sokoine University of Agriculture and a PhD candidate in Crop Science, specializing in Crop Improvement. He holds an MSc in Seed Technology and Business and a BSc in General Agriculture.

His academic and research work spans crop production, seed science, soil science, crop improvement, experimental design, crop health, agricultural value chains and quantitative analysis. He has experience in research coordination, field data collection, data management, statistical analysis, scientific writing, teaching and student mentorship. His technical profile also includes Python, molecular and bioinformatics workflows, machine learning, predictive modelling, neural networks, artificial intelligence, and computer and ICT tools.
